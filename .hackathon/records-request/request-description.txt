Pursuant to the California Public Records Act (Gov. Code § 7920.000 et seq.), I request electronic copies of the following records relating to street outreach and homelessness-services capacity in downtown San Diego. Native electronic format is preferred (Excel/CSV where the record is a spreadsheet, PDF otherwise), and rolling production is welcome as records become available.

1. Street outreach program agreements and staffing. For street outreach or coordinated outreach programs serving downtown San Diego that are administered or funded by SDHC (including but not limited to agreements with PATH, Alpha Project, City Net, Downtown San Diego Partnership, or successor providers), for January 1, 2023 to the present:
   (a) the current executed agreements, scopes of work, and any exhibit, budget, or attachment stating staffing plans, positions, full-time-equivalent (FTE) counts, schedules, shift structures, or staff hours; and
   (b) monthly or quarterly performance, activity, or outcome reports submitted by the provider(s), including outreach contacts or encounters, engagements, and shelter or housing placements, with any geographic breakdown (downtown, community planning area, or neighborhood) that already exists in the record.

2. Homelessness Response Center activity reports. HRC monthly activity reports for February 2025 through the present, in the same form as the "FY24 HRC Activity Report" and "FY25 HRC Activity Report (July-Jan)" spreadsheets previously released in response to Request 25-52.

3. Outreach deployment records. Any staffing plan, organizational chart, deployment schedule, or zone assignment document showing outreach worker positions, FTEs, shifts, or assigned coverage areas for downtown street outreach services in effect at any time from January 1, 2024 to the present.

To assist your search, an attached reference sheet lists the related agreements and prior releases I have already reviewed on your portal (Agreements HHI-21-17, HHI-24-23, and HHI-25-51, and the Request 25-52 release), so those need not be reproduced.

If any portion of a responsive record is exempt, please release the segregable remainder (Gov. Code § 7922.525). Records containing client-level personal information may be produced with that information redacted or excluded; aggregate reports are preferred. This request is made for noncommercial civic research in connection with a public-interest data project on downtown homelessness measurement.
