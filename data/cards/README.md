Source, quality, and model metadata cards. Committed: these are the human-readable
provenance record for everything the product publishes.

The ledger also records reference-only system context and the separate public
monitoring lineage. A ledger entry does not make a source eligible for the
forecast or planner; each lineage declares its permitted and excluded uses.
